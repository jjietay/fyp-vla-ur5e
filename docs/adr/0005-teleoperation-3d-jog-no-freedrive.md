# Demonstrations are driven with a 3D jog device through servo_to_pose, never freedrive

Decided 23 Sep 2026; the device is still to confirm (A15). With freedrive, the operator's hands are in both camera views on every frame, and a visuomotor policy can learn that cue, which is absent at test time. A leader arm was rejected because a 5 DOF leader onto a 6 DOF UR5e is not a solved mapping. Inspect the frames of the first five episodes before recording the rest.
