Reach testThe arm hovers 2 cm above a reach-test cross that the camera located (circle: the TCP). I jog it over the cross, and the jog, read over RTDE, is the miss.
Sees the shift the fit hides
4
Reach correctionA yaw and a shift are fitted to the misses and written on top of the ball fit. The correction is empirical, so it must be tested again.
Removes the bias the reach test found
5
Second reach testThe same test at new crosses that the correction was not fitted to. A pass makes the calibration validated for the session.
Gives a validated calibration
Any later calibration step makes the calibration unvalidated again, so no trial runs until step 5 passes.
