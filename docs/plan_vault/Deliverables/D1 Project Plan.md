# D1 Project Plan

**Submit 14 Sep 2026 to supervisor. Worth 7 marks.**

A short plan and strategy report, required about six weeks into the project. Assessed on whether the research activities are planned with minimal supervision and whether the plan is clear and reasonable with proper background knowledge.

## Required contents

* main objectives of the project
* background
* the proposed way of carrying out the project tasks
* a proposed weekly schedule **in chart form**, which is a stated requirement and not optional

## How to build it fast

Almost all of this already exists in the technical plan and can be lifted:

* objectives and background come from the scope statement, which was written to be reused verbatim
* the approach section is the workstream list, see [[Workstreams Overview]]
* the chart is [[Schedule]] reformatted as a Gantt chart rather than a table

## Watch out

The examiner is allocated based on the StaffLink title and summary, not on this document. If the recorded scope still says pick and place, fix that separately and before Week 8. That is tracked in [[Open Actions]].
