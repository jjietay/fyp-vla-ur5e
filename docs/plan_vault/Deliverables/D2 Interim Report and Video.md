# D2 Interim Report and Video

**Submit 10 Nov 2026 to supervisor and examiner. Worth 13 marks, plus the video.**

The single most important date in 2026. It is the one deliverable that is both finished and submitted this year.

## Specification

* 5 to 10 A4 pages, which is short, so cutting matters more than writing
* Turnitin plagiarism check **before** submitting to supervisor and examiner
* a separate video file, 2 to 3 minutes, summarising the project
* a form acknowledging any use of generative AI

## Suggested structure

* introduction: background, purpose, scope, objectives, the project timeline, and the purpose of the report itself
* work conducted: what is done, the achievement and effort, and problems encountered
* future work: what is in progress, what remains, and any changes to the original requirements
* conclusion: an honest assessment of progress

## The advantage of finishing early

By 10 Nov the evaluation is complete, so this report carries **results rather than progress**. Most interim reports say "I have built half a pipeline". This one can say "both architectures run and here is the comparison". Against a criterion that rewards good content, understanding and logic, that is where the 13 marks are.

The video should show the robot working. A 2 minute clip of a spoken command producing real motion is worth more than any slide. Record it during [[W7 Evaluation and Write Up]] while the cell is already set up.

The scope changes since the original proposal belong in the future work section, per [[Task Tiers]].
