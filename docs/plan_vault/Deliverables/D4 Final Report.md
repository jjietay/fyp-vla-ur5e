**Submit 09 Apr 2027 to supervisor and examiner. Finished by 9 Nov 2026.**

## Hard formatting requirements

* main body 40 to 60 pages, excluding charts, diagrams, appendices, tables and references
* exceeding the limit can mean being asked to resubmit a condensed version
* Times New Roman 12, one and a half spacing
* margins 35 mm left, 30 mm top, bottom and right

## Where the marks are

Split across writing quality and references (11), introduction, theory, review, discussion and conclusion (12), and shared with the demo and oral (17). Full breakdown in [[Assessment Mapping]].

## Framing

Lead with the interface, then use the comparison to answer what has to sit behind it. The official project summary promises a user interface enabling more intuitive human robot interaction, so the report should open on the user speaking and the robot acting, not on two pipelines being benchmarked.

The high standard criterion explicitly asks for limitations of the current work to be highlighted, so [[Risks]] and the failure analysis are not padding, they are marked content.
