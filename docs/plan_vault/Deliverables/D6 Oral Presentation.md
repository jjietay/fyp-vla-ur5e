# D6 Oral Presentation

**10 to 12 May 2027. 15 minutes presenting, 10 minutes questions.**

Attended and assessed by both supervisor and examiner. Venue announced nearer the date.

Assessed on logical sequence, ability to highlight major outcomes, quality of explanation, effort in slide design, and answering questions. The high standard is answering *most* questions, so anticipate them.

* why SmolVLA rather than a larger VLA

* whether the comparison is fair given the architectures need different amounts of data
* what speech recognition contributes, given it is off the shelf
* what would change with a second arm, or a mobile base
